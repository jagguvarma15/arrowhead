# Support handbook

## Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within five business days of
approval. A refund request must include the order number and the reason for the
return. Partial refunds are available for opened items.

## Shipping

Standard shipping takes three to seven business days. Expedited shipping arrives
within two business days and is offered at checkout for an additional fee. A
tracking link is emailed when the order leaves the warehouse.

## Accounts

A locked account is released after a support agent verifies the owner's
identity. Password resets are self-service from the sign-in page, and a reset
link expires one hour after it is sent.
